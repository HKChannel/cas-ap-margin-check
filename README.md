# CAS AP Margin & Stack Readiness Check

A single-page app in one file (`index.html`) with no dependencies. It's the companion tool for an edition of the LinkedIn newsletter **AI in Finance, Done Right**: **"Why Standardizing on NetSuite, Not Just Buying AI, Let This Outsourced Accounting Firm Put 78% of Client AP on Autopilot"**.

## Features
- **Tab 1, Margin case:** Enter your client invoices a month, minutes per invoice today and per touched invoice with AI, staff cost, number of clients, average monthly retainer and staff hours per client. Pick a no-touch rate yourself or use one of three presets from the edition: Ramp 38% (a 12,000-invoice multi-client firm), Pilot 67% and Production 78% (Countsy, firm-reported). See hours back a month, cost saved a year, margin points on retainer revenue and how many more clients that capacity covers without hiring.
- **Tab 2, Stack readiness:** Six steps to AI-enabled, human-led AP, from one core ERP for every client to a named owner who signs off every ledger. You get a readiness dial, the biggest gaps and a margin case you can copy.

The demo figures are made up. Countsy results are as reported by the firm and its vendor. Everything runs in the browser and nothing is uploaded.
